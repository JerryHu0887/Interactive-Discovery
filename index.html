How to Investigate

You are an archive researcher deciding what belongs in the official record of October 9, 2026. A cancelled public event does not necessarily mean the building was unused.

  • Use the Theatre Map to travel directly to a room. Gold means unexplored; green means its key evidence was discovered.
  • Inspect objects and read both sides of any document that can be turned over.
  • Use Evidence Connections on the Investigation Board to explain two pairs of records. The third comparison and several records are optional.
  • Turn the A12 ticket over. A document's back may change its meaning.
  • Reconstruct the stage by interpreting the prop inventory and lighting cues, not by following a numbered button list.
  • Use Need a Hint? for up to three levels of help. Hints never delete your progress.
  • Reconstruction demonstrates a sequence; the recovered programme establishes its meaning. Restart clears your progress.
